Our story

Rooted in Huye.
Driven by creativity.

THARCILLE FAHION HOUSE is envisioned as a student-owned fashion design, garment production and retail business, managed by students of Journalism and Communication. We see clothes as design objects — and every garment as an opportunity to tell a story.

Through fashion photography, branding, digital communication and storytelling, our students bring fresh energy to fashion entrepreneurship.
